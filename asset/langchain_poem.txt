Across the sea of data vast,
Where prompts are built and queries cast,
There lies a bridge of modern art,
Connecting language to its heart:
**LangChain** weaves the threads of thought,
To capture wisdom long besought.

With **Document Loaders** leading the way,
They gather raw files day by day—
PDFs, webpages, markdown text,
Preparing all for what comes next.
They parse the chaos, clean the stream,
To feed the artificial dream.

Then **Splitters** slice the words with care,
Creating chunks for search to share.
Through **Embeddings** turned to math and space,
Each concept finds its vector place,
Stored deep within a **Vector DB**,
For instant, rich retrieval, free.

When questions rise, the **Retriever** knows
Where hidden understanding grows;
It fetches context, clear and bright,
To give the **LLM** its sight.
No longer guessing in the dark,
The prompt receives a grounded spark.

From simple chains to complex flows,
The architecture brightly glowers—
A framework crafted, smart and grand,
To put AI's full power in your hand!
